Connection & safety

Choose Connect ESP32 to begin.

The buzzer is a small sounder, not a speaker. A passive buzzer can play pitches but cannot reproduce rich music. Keep the buzzer connected as in your existing hardware and avoid driving a high-current speaker directly from a GPIO.
